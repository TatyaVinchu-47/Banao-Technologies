**Re:** Refunds: the real number, why it rose, and one leak to close

**1. The quarter is Rs 12.8 lakh, not a crore.**
The export mixes two units. Old Freshdesk tickets (before 14 Sep 2025) store refunds in paise, so they read
100 times too big. On top of that, 638 tickets appear twice because of the migration. Corrected, Apr–Jun 2026
refunds are **Rs 12.80 lakh on 473 tickets**. The helpdesk's "about Rs 11 lakh" leaves out refunds on
auto-closed and still-open tickets (about Rs 2 lakh). The *Reconciliation* sheet goes from your figure to
ours, quarter by quarter, to the rupee.

**2. Refunds rose mainly because tickets rose.**
Refunds doubled, from Rs 6.1 lakh (Jan–Mar 2025) to Rs 12.8 lakh. Tickets also doubled, from 1,053 to 2,277.
About one ticket in five carries a refund, and the average refund is Rs 2,700–3,000, both steady. The exception
is product faults, which policy says should be repaired or replaced, not refunded. Those refunds went from
Rs 0.6 lakh to Rs 2–2.6 lakh a quarter from October 2025, the "stop arguing" change Priya describes.
Satisfaction scores in this data, though, are flat at about 3.5/5. We could not find the 0.4 rise.

**3. The reason codes do not yet say "for what".**
46% of last quarter's refund money was coded "Goodwill / Other". That is the first option in the dropdown,
so it is the default when an agent doesn't choose. The agents' notes give a specific, ordinary reason for
78% of it: a delayed return refund, a double payment, a cancellation. The board pack shows the chosen code
and the actual reason side by side.

**4. Where money is lost: refund *and* replacement.**
Policy forbids giving both for the same order. Last quarter it happened **76 times, on 16% of refunds**, and
360 times over 18 months. The "replacement issued" tick-box missed 199 of the 360, which is why it went
unnoticed. It is not the Returns Desk (5% of its refunds). It is Escalations & Warranty (59%), Chat (28%) and
Logistics (27%). Notes often say "team lead aware" or "customer threatened social media", so treat this as a
process gap before treating it as an individual one.

**Goal: cut refund-plus-replacement from 16% of refunds to under 2% by Oct–Dec 2026, saving about Rs 1.2
lakh a quarter (Rs 5 lakh a year).** This is costed conservatively, at the policy's own figure of product
cost plus Rs 340 shipping for each replacement that should not have gone out. Three changes would get there:
- block closing a ticket that has both a refund and a replacement without team-lead sign-off;
- start the reason dropdown blank instead of on "Goodwill";
- send a weekly exception list from our tool to team leads and to you.

**5. How sure we are.**
The totals are arithmetic, and every check passes. The note reading we checked by hand on 139 tickets. On
100 picked at random, the tool agreed with us every time, and the tick-box was wrong 12 times. We wrote the
rules and the checks ourselves, so before the 24th we suggest Neha's team re-checks 50 flagged tickets.
When a note contradicts itself, the tool sends it to a person and leaves it out of the totals.

**6. Left out on purpose.** The Rs 350 late-response credits (a separate cost line), staffing and repeat
contacts. Sheets marked INTERNAL name agents. They are for team leads, not for the board.
